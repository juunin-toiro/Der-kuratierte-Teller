# Project brief
**Status:** CONFIRMED product intent; technical implementation PROPOSED.

## Vision
Der kuratierte Teller: a calm, warm, precise, science-informed German-language nutrition and recipe platform. Combine evidence-aware education with enjoyable, affordable, time-efficient recipes and practical tools. Brand motif: bee and hexagon; recipe cards as honeycomb cells.

## Topics
Epinutrition / epigenetic nutrition; macronutrients; fibre; micronutrients; anti-inflammatory eating; polyphenols; antioxidants; gastrophysics / sensory experience.

## Product promise
Clarity: what is in a recipe, in what quantity, where to get it. Health context: goals and dietary needs. Ease: practical everyday cooking. Delight: tasty swaps without unnecessary compromise.

## Audiences (from project planning PDF; hypotheses, not validated market statistics)
Primary concepts: Blood Sugar Balancer, Protein Foodie with Goals, Time-Crunch Cook, Sensitive Gourmet. Other explored personas: Gut-friendly Baker, Healthy-Swap Lover, Curious Homecook. Avoid treating illustrative segment percentages as research findings.

## Affiliate candidates (not verified programs)
Lidl, Migros, Coop, iHerb, HSN, Vilgain, Powerfood, Redcare, keto.ch. Availability, commissions, product feeds, and legal requirements remain OPEN.

## Non-goals for MVP (PROPOSED)
No required accounts, subscriptions, clinical decision support, CGM integrations, full commerce, or always-on database.
