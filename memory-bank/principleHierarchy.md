# Principle hierarchy
1. **Safety and truth:** never fabricate nutrition evidence, clinical benefits, allergy safety or technical verification.
2. **User-approved decisions:** preserve confirmed design and product constraints.
3. **Accessibility:** readable contrast, semantic controls, keyboard and reduced motion.
4. **Simplicity and maintainability:** static-first, minimal dependencies, one source of truth.
5. **Sustainability:** measured transfer/compute efficiency rather than unverified claims.
6. **Design freedom:** reusable primitives, no unnecessary framework or theme lock-in.
7. **Delight:** warm, clear, honeycomb-inspired editorial experience.

When principles conflict, explain the tradeoff and ask for approval on material changes.
