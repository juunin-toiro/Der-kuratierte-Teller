# Product context
## User jobs
- Discover delicious recipes fitting time, budget, food restrictions and goals.
- Understand macro/micro/fibre information and the strength of supporting evidence.
- Adjust servings and potentially swap ingredients without losing clarity.
- Move seamlessly from educational article to practical recipe and shopping list.

## Product experiences
1. Editorial learning articles with illustrations and links to actionable recipes.
2. Recipe discovery with honeycomb-inspired cards and clear filters.
3. Cooklang-authored recipes rendered in tabular form, with accessible alternatives.
4. Later: nutrition calculator, personalised planning and member functionality (not approved for MVP).

## Content integrity
Use clear references, publication/review dates and evidence labels. Distinguish mechanism studies from human outcomes. Avoid promises that a food treats disease or that a quiz diagnoses deficiency. Diabetes and allergy labels must not imply guaranteed safety or predictable glucose responses.
