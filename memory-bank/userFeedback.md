# User feedback and preferences
- Design should feel calm, light, warm and precise, not generic fitness/medical UI.
- Maximal visual freedom and easy changes matter more than a conventional blog theme.
- Low cost, low maintenance and Green IT are first-class constraints.
- Bee and hexagon are the visual organizing motif.
- Cooklang and tabular recipe display are especially interesting.
- Do not change the specified palette, typography or contrast policy without asking.

Update with dated, concrete feedback only; avoid inferred personal traits.
